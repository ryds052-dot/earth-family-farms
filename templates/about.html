100 Acres of Living Heritage

Located in the lush coastal highlands of Ocho Rios, St. Ann, Jamaica, Earth Family Farms is a sanctuary dedicated to organic cultivation, holistic wellness, and sustainable community living.

The Land & Water

Where the Rivers Meet

Our sanctuary is blessed with over 300 mature coconut palms, seasonal black sapote, soursop, mango, banana, pineapple, and jackfruit groves, and the pristine spring waters of Collier Gully.

Whether you join us for a guided botanical tour, spend a night under the stars in The Queen Cabin, or claim a private parcel in our highland subdivision, you become part of an enduring ecological vision.
