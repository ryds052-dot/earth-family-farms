Ocho Rios • St. Ann • Jamaica

A Living Heritage

Earth Family Farms is a 100-acre sanctuary in the coastal highlands, where tropical agriculture, spring waters, and a vision for mindful community living come together.

Farm • Nature • Community

Where the Rivers Meet

Across our 100-acre sanctuary, more than 300 coconut palms rise among groves of black sapote, soursop, jackfruit, mango, banana, and pineapple. At Collier Gully, clear spring waters bring the landscape to life.

Here in Ocho Rios, St. Ann, Jamaica, guided farm experiences and time in nature connect guests with the land and an enduring vision for sustainable community living.
