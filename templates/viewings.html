Come and walk it.

Ninety minutes, one host, no group. You see the parcels, the water and the access road at your own pace.

What happens

  1. 01You call. We agree a time and tell you exactly where to meet on Lady Musgrave Road.
  2. 02We drive up together and walk the boundary of the parcels you asked about.
  3. 03You see the spring, the canopy and the view. We answer everything on site, not by email.
  4. 04If it fits, we send the parcel sheet and the terms the same day.

Before you come

Closed shoes, long trousers, water. The track is graded but it is a hill. Morning slots are cooler and the light is better.
