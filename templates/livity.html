The land

Above Ocho Rios, held in forest and spring water.

Collier sits on the ridge at Bonham Spring, St. Ann — minutes inland from Ocho Rios and Tower Isle, with cool highland air, a natural gully watercourse, and direct main-road access.

The groundwork is already behind it: surveyed, laid out around the working farm and canopy, and submitted for parish review. You are not looking at raw bush.

Architecture & Livity

Stone below, hardwood above, glass to the drop.

The covenant sets the material language, not your floor plan: local limestone, stained hardwood, brass, no ornament. Setbacks keep every home low enough for the canopy to stay the horizon.

Architectural drawings and a contractor shortlist come with the plan. You are not handed an empty slope and a set of rules.

The village

A handful of villas and a fire pavilion. Nothing else.

Homes step down an elevated hardwood walk toward the gully and the shared gathering space at the head of the ridge.
