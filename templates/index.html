A little more life

Rooted in the land. Open to you.

Earth Family Farms is a place to pause. Our family-run farm sits in the green hills of St. Ann, where mornings are filled with golden light, fresh coffee, and the sound of the breeze moving through the trees.

Come for a weekend, stay for a season of memories. We are building a gentler way to travel—one that gives back to the earth and leaves room for wonder.
