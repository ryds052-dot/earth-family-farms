About the farm

A slower rhythm, rooted in nature.

Earth Family Farms offers a warm, grounded escape in the green hills of St. Ann. From farm-to-table meals and river adventures to soulful cultural experiences, each stay is shaped around rest, connection, and authentic Jamaican living.

Whether you are joining us for a weekend reset or a longer wellness retreat, you will find a peaceful place to breathe, explore, and reconnect with both nature and yourself.
