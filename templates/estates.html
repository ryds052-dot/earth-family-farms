01Why here

A ridge this close to the coast is not reproducible.

Coastal St. Ann is built out at the shoreline and climbing inland fast. Land above Ocho Rios with an ocean aspect, live spring water and mature hardwood is finite — the parcels that exist are the parcels that will ever exist.

The slow, technical work is already behind it: surveyed, laid out, engineered, submitted. A buyer is not paying for raw bush.

08Questions
Has it been approved?

Not yet — submitted and in progress. Sold pre-development, which is why the entry sits below approved-land pricing.

What if approval is refused?

Deposits are held against the parcel and returned if the plan does not clear. Nothing is payable to completion before approval.

Must I build within a set time?

No. The covenant sets what may be built, not when.

Can foreign buyers purchase?

Yes. Non-residents hold Jamaican freehold through the standard transfer process; your attorney files it.

Water and power?

Spring water is on the estate. Power and connectivity are confirmed parcel by parcel in due diligence.
