Why here

A ridge this close to the coast is not reproducible.

Coastal St. Ann is built out at the shoreline and climbing inland fast. Land above Ocho Rios with an ocean aspect, live spring water and mature hardwood is finite — the ground that exists is the ground that will ever exist.
